This project presents an interactive Stock Performance Analytics Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel using historical stock market data of major Tata Group companies. The dashboard enables investors and analysts to evaluate stock performance through key metrics such as price trends, trading volume, volatility, and drawdown risk. By combining interactive visualizations, KPIs, and filtering capabilities, the dashboard provides actionable insights into investment performance, market participation, and risk exposure across multiple Tata Group stocks.

Dataset:

The dataset contains historical stock market data for major Tata Group companies from 2006 to 2025
TCS,Tata Motors,Tata Power,Tata Consumers,Titan,Trent,Voltas,Tata Communications,Tata Technologies,Tejas Networks,Indian Hotels,Tata Chemicals

Business Questions Solved:
1. Which Tata Group stocks generated the strongest long-term performance?
Finding:
Tata india hotels have generated the strongest long term perfromance driven by strong post pandemic recovery.

2. Which stocks attract the highest investor participation?
Finding:
Tata Motors,Tata Power and Tata India Hotels recorded the highest traded volumes among tata group stocks indicating strongest investor participation and highest liquidity in these counters.This suggests great market interests.

3. Which stocks are the most volatile?
Finding:
Volatility analysis shows that TCS,Trent,Titan and Tejas networks are the most volatile tata group stocks indicating higher price flctuations and strongest market sensitivity. In contrast Tata power and Tata india hotels exhibits relatively low volatility suggesting more stable and defensive price behaviour.

4. Which stocks experienced the largest downside risk?
Finding:
Trent experienced the largest downside risk among Tata group stocks with a maximum drawdown of approximately 20% followed by tejas networks at 19% and tata motors at 18% indicating higher capital erosion during market downturns.

5. How have Tata Group stocks evolved over time?
Finding:
The tata groups stocks exhibit clear structural evlutions overtime.Early years shows gradual steady growth followed by a sharp acceleration after 2020.The period between 2021-2024 reflects strong momentum across most stocks,driven by post pandemic recovery and sectoral expansions.

Key Insights:
•Liquidity Leaders
Tata Motors and Tata Power recorded the highest trading volumes, indicating strong investor participation and market liquidity.

•High-Risk Stocks
TCS, Trent, and Tejas Network exhibited the highest average volatility, making them potentially attractive for aggressive investors while carrying greater risk.

•Risk Management Perspective
Drawdown analysis revealed that Trent and Tejas Network experienced larger declines from previous peaks, suggesting higher downside exposure during market corrections.

•Long-Term Growth Trend
The overall stock price trend indicates substantial value creation across Tata Group companies, particularly after 2020, reflecting strong market confidence and business expansion.

•Investment Decision Support
The dashboard enables investors to evaluate stocks from three dimensions:
Performance,Risk,Liquidity
