# Tata Group Stock Performance Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Stock Performance Analytics Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel using historical stock market data of major Tata Group companies. The dashboard enables investors and analysts to evaluate stock performance through key metrics such as price trends, trading volume, volatility, and drawdown risk. By combining interactive visualizations, KPIs, and filtering capabilities, it provides actionable insights into investment performance, market participation, and risk exposure across multiple Tata Group stocks.

## Dataset

Historical stock market data for major Tata Group companies from 2006 to 2025:

TCS, Tata Motors, Tata Power, Tata Consumer Products, Titan, Trent, Voltas, Tata Communications, Tata Technologies, Tejas Networks, Tata India Hotels, Tata Chemicals


## Business Questions & Findings

**1. Which Tata Group stocks generated the strongest long-term performance?**
Tata India Hotels generated the strongest long-term performance, driven by a strong post-pandemic recovery.

**2. Which stocks attract the highest investor participation?**
Tata Motors, Tata Power, and Tata India Hotels recorded the highest traded volumes among Tata Group stocks, indicating the strongest investor participation and highest liquidity in these counters.

**3. Which stocks are the most volatile?**
Volatility analysis shows that TCS, Trent, Titan, and Tejas Networks are the most volatile Tata Group stocks, indicating higher price fluctuations and stronger market sensitivity. In contrast, Tata Power and Tata India Hotels exhibit relatively low volatility, suggesting more stable, defensive price behaviour — notable given Tata Power's high trading volume.

**4. Which stocks experienced the largest downside risk?**
Trent experienced the largest downside risk among Tata Group stocks, with a maximum drawdown of approximately 20%, followed by Tejas Networks at 19% and Tata Motors at 18%, indicating higher capital erosion during market downturns.

**5. How have Tata Group stocks evolved over time?**
Tata Group stocks show clear structural evolution over time. Early years show gradual, steady growth, followed by a sharp acceleration after 2020. The period between 2021–2024 reflects strong momentum across most stocks, driven by post-pandemic recovery and sectoral expansion.

---

## Key Insights

- **Liquidity Leaders** — Tata Motors and Tata Power recorded the highest trading volumes, indicating strong investor participation and market liquidity.
- **High-Risk Stocks** — TCS, Trent, and Tejas Networks exhibited the highest average volatility, making them potentially attractive for aggressive investors while carrying greater risk.
- **Risk Management Perspective** — Drawdown analysis revealed that Trent and Tejas Networks experienced larger declines from previous peaks, suggesting higher downside exposure during market corrections.
- **Long-Term Growth Trend** — The overall stock price trend indicates substantial value creation across Tata Group companies, particularly after 2020, reflecting strong market confidence and business expansion.
- **Investment Decision Support** — The dashboard enables investors to evaluate stocks across three dimensions: **Performance, Risk, and Liquidity.**

## How to Use

1. Open `tata_stock_dashboard.xlsx` in Excel.
2. Use the slicers/filters on the dashboard sheet to select a stock or date range.
3. KPI cards update automatically to show price trend, volume, volatility, and drawdown for the selected stock(s).
